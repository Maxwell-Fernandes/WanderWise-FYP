## Abstract

To solve the problem of low accuracy of traditional travel route recommendation
algorithm, a travel route recommendation algorithm based on interest theme and
distance matching is proposed in this paper. Firstly, the real historical travel
footprints of users are obtained through analysis. Then, the user’s preferences of
interest theme and distance matching are proposed based on the user’s stay in each
scenic spot. Finally, the optimal travel route calculation method is designed under
